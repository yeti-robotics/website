Frostbyte stacked totes on an air-powered stacker and collected cap bins with a
giant roller claw on a gravity wrist. A large arm on two Mini CIMs moved the claw
up and down.

## 2016 configuration

We converted the drive to pneumatic wheels and six CIMs, and turned the arm into
a ball intake that could operate the drawbridge, sally port, cheval de frise and
every other defense except the low bar. A belt-fed intake ran to a shooter, where
a CIM and a cylinder with a drum cymbal on the end ejected the ball through the
flywheel into the high goal.

## 2017 configuration

Back to a traction wheel tank drive with corner omnis and six CIMs, plus a roller
bar gear intake, pneumatic ejection doors for placing gears, and a velcro climber
on two RS775s.
