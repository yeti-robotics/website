We chose a six-wheel West Coast drivetrain with two sets of WCP four-inch
traction wheels in the back and one set of omnis in the front to help steering,
with no drop center for maximum stability. WCP Flipped CIM two-speed gearboxes on
Falcon 500s got us to a realistic top speed of 14.27 feet per second.

The intake picks cargo off the ground and from the designated start positions,
and can also receive balls from the human player station. The tower stores what
the intake picks up and delivers it to the shooter on NEO 550 driven rollers.

The shooter is the most versatile part of the robot — three WCP solid roller
wheels on two Falcon 500s, on a NEO 550 turret, letting us adjust the ball's
trajectory in almost any direction. The climber reaches the mid-rung on a chain
driven by two Falcon 500s through a custom gearbox.

Aurora went 39-17-1 overall in 2022 counting unofficial play.
