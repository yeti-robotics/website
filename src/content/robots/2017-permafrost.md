Permafrost picked up gears with an active claw intake at the front, stored them
in a compartment at the back, and placed them through air-cylinder ejection
doors.

The climber used velcro on both the winch and the end of the rope, powered by two
RS775s on 30:1 VersaPlanetary gearboxes.

Permafrost went 24-31-1 overall in 2017 counting unofficial play.
