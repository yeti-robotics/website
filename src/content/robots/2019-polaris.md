We chose tank drive on Polaris for pushing power and for how it handled while
playing defense.

The hatch panel intake was inspired by the jaws of life: a one-inch stroke piston
driving 3D printed claws and linkages. Two arcade buttons on the front closed the
disk intake and retracted the two pistons that extended it forward.

The cascade elevator ran four programmed stages and could reach the third level
of the rocket. Programming used vision, limit switches, encoders and line
followers to make the autonomous routines consistent. The bumpers were wood and
pool noodles.
