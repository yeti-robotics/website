Black Ice ran a pneumatic six-wheel West Coast drive with shifters, 19 ft/s in
high gear. A variable intake arm both grabbed boulders and damaged Stronghold's
defenses, with a roller bar built into the intake that pinched the ball against
the bumper.

The climber was an extending scissor lift deployed by stretched speargun tubing —
six seconds to its full six-foot height — held by a winch with an air brake so it
could not fall. At 14 inches tall it could quickly damage every defense from the
front except the drawbridge and sally port, both of which it could back-door.

Black Ice also competed as part of THOR, the Thundering Herd of Robots.
