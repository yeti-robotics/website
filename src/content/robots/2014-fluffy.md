Fluffy launched yoga balls with an air-powered catapult driven by four cylinders
stretching speargun tubing. Short passes ran 1 to 5 feet with or without air
assist; long passes reached about 35 feet for trussing, though we took no high
goal shots.

A catch frame on top let us receive balls shot at us by other robots, and a
plexiglass frame blocked shots and kept the ball contained so it could not be
stolen.
