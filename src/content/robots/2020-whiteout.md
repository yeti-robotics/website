Whiteout's intake used mecanum wheels we made in-house on the roller bar,
designed around a touch-it-own-it philosophy. The single flywheel shooter ran a
3:1 pulley ratio off two 775 Pros, built mostly from sheet metal apart from a
3D printed hood.

After the season was cancelled we kept working on the robot, improving the
electrical system and the climber to teach new rookies and to prepare for
offseason competitions. The climber runs two Falcon 500s through a custom gearbox
and has a brake that holds the robot in the air.
