Chompa started the season on an eight-wheel drive and was converted to six-wheel
partway through. A multi-roller urethane belt system pulled basketballs in and
fed a muzzle-loaded articulating shooter, angled by an AndyMark PG71 winch.

Inside the shooter, a Denso-driven belt system and two belt-driven CIMs fired the
ball. An articulating pneumatic arm dropped the bridges for balancing.
