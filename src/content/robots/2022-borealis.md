Borealis is YETI Robotics' first swerve drivetrain robot, built in the 2022
offseason on Swerve Drive Specialties MK4i modules powered by Falcon 500s.

It ranked 9th with a 4-4-0 record at Doyenne, the offseason competition it was
built for. Everything we learned about swerve on this robot carried into the
competition robots that followed.
