Subzero was our first turreted robot. A NEO 550 drove the turret, with physical
limit switches setting the boundaries and a magnetic limit switch handling
zeroing. The shooter ran two four-inch flywheels off a pair of Falcon 500s, with
a NEO 550 adjusting the hood.

There is no awards list for 2021. The season was COVID.
