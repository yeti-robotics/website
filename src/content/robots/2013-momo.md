MoMo excelled in autonomous, usually making all three shots in the high goal.

The frisbee chamber sat vertically, which made loading from the middle slot
forgiving — feeding happened on the opposite side from shooting. The launcher
used two tires spinning at high speed, with Hall Effect encoders on the hubs so
we could control launcher speed accurately.

A two-speed transmission gave us speed when we needed it and enough torque to
push other robots when we did not.
