Avalanche's intake arm was laser cut from quarter-inch aluminium sheet and
pivoted on an RS775 through a 330:1 gearbox. Two roller bars, each with a pair of
35A compliant wheels chained to RS775s, let it pick up a power cube from any
orientation.

The continuous elevator raised that arm seven feet in two seconds, controlled
with encoders and limit switches.

Avalanche went 39-37-0 overall in 2018 counting unofficial play.
