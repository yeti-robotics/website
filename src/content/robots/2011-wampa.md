Wampa drove on mecanum wheels and hung logo pieces with a scope gripper on a
four-bar linkage. The arm ran off a single spring-assisted Denso door motor, and
a three-stage lift on industrial door slides put game pieces on the high pegs.

Later in the season we added an rs380 on a belt drive to place pieces faster.
The minibot deployed off stretched rubber tubing on drawer slides, released by a
servo, and climbed in roughly one second using hard drive magnets and two motors.
