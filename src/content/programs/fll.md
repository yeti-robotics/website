## What we do

YETI students mentor four FLL teams each fall. Every team gets two of our
students on site every week, plus a mentor who handles the parts and the
paperwork so the coaches can focus on the kids.

## Who it is for

Students in grades 4 through 8. No experience needed, and no cost to the
families — the team covers registration and kits.
